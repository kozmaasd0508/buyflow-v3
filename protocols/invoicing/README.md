# Invoicing protocols

Provider-specific evidence for Billingo, Számlázz.hu and future invoicing systems.

Profiles should distinguish invoice, proforma/díjbekérő, quote and delivery-note documents; extract invoice/order identities, amount, payment method, PDF/link metadata and preserve provenance. Invoice-provider or PDF evidence may outrank a generic merchant word such as `számla`, but exact Purchase linking remains the job of the existing document resolver.
